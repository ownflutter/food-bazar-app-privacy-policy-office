Privacy Policy

Food Bazar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy describes how Food Bazar ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, processes, and protects your personal data when you use the Food Bazar mobile application and services. We are dedicated to respecting your privacy while providing an intuitive, interactive marketplace for table bookings, dining orders, live food tracking, map-based discovery, and social event sharing.

🍽️

1. About Food Bazar & Services

Food Bazar is a comprehensive digital dining, food ordering, and event-booking platform. Through our mobile application, you can:

  • Discover & Explore: Find nearby restaurants, cafes, and community centers via interactive map navigation.
  • Pre-order & Table Booking: Reserve tables with customized food choices and pre-set menus ahead of your arrival.
  • Dine-In QR Scan: Scan QR codes placed on restaurant tables using your camera to instantly view digital menus and place dine-in orders directly.
  • Live Food Updates: Track real-time kitchen preparation status, delivery dispatch, and live order milestones.
  • Online & Digital Payments: Pay for orders, advance reservation deposits, or dining bills securely via integrated payment gateways.
  • Social Invitations & Event Sharing: Host and organize group gatherings, weddings, birthdays, or anniversary events at community halls/restaurants, and invite friends and family through shareable in-app links or cards.
📋

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Profile: Full name, phone number, email address, profile picture (optional), and encrypted passwords.
  • Pre-Orders & Food Preferences: Selected food dishes, ingredient notes, dietary preferences, spice level customizations, and special culinary requests.
  • Table & Venue Bookings: Date, seating time, table counts, guest counts, and special seating arrangements.
  • Event Hosting Details: Event titles, categories (e.g., Wedding, Birthday, Anniversary, Corporate Party), community center or restaurant hall selections, and event schedules.
  • Friends & Family Invitations: Contact details or phone numbers you input to share digital invitations, and RSVP responses submitted by your guests.
  • Reviews, Ratings & Feedback: Comments, food photos you upload to reviews, and customer support queries.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically & Permissions

  • Precise & Approximate Location (GPS & Maps): Collected only with your explicit permission to plot nearby restaurants on the map, measure driving/walking distance, and display accurate estimated arrival/delivery times.
  • Camera & QR Scanner: Accessed exclusively to scan table QR codes at partner venues to load instant menus and associate orders with your specific table. We never record, capture, or store background video or unauthorized photos.
  • Device & Telemetry Data: Hardware model, operating system version, IP address, unique device identifiers, and crash logs to maintain app stability.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Used to alert you about real-time food preparation status, booking confirmations, invite acceptances, and event updates.

2.3 Financial & Payment Data

Payments are handled by secure, PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateways (e.g., bKash, Nagad, Visa, Mastercard). We never record, access, or store your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or mobile financial service PINs. We only retain the transaction reference ID, payment timestamp, and transaction status.

⚙️

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Execution: To confirm table reservations, relay pre-selected menu items to restaurant kitchens, and manage venue bookings for events.
  • Map & Location Services: To showcase curated restaurant listings within your geographical radius and calculate travel routes.
  • Real-time Food Tracking: To push instant status alerts as your order moves from "Order Received" to "Preparing", "Ready for Pickup", or "Served".
  • Social Gathering & Invitation Management: To generate invitation links, manage RSVP guest lists for your weddings, birthdays, or celebrations, and notify invited attendees.
  • Safety & Fraud Prevention: To verify accounts via OTP, authenticate dine-in QR orders, prevent fake reservations, and resolve disputes.
  • App Optimization: To fix crashes, enhance UI/UX workflows, and ensure reliable performance across devices.
🤝

4. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell or rent your personal data to advertisers. We share information strictly on a need-to-know basis as outlined below:

RecipientInformation Shared & Purpose
Partner Restaurants & Venues Name, phone number, customized food selections, seating time, table ID, and event reservation details to prepare food and reserve spaces.
Invited Friends & Family Event host name, venue map location, event date/time, and celebration details displayed on invitation cards sent by you.
Map & Location Providers (e.g., Google Maps API) Coordinates and device location requests to render dynamic map markers, nearby outlets, and route calculations.
Payment Service Providers Order totals, currency, and invoice references required to complete encrypted checkout and process refunds.
Infrastructure & Notification Vendors (e.g., Firebase) Encrypted tokens and device logs to dispatch real-time food tracking alerts and order notifications.
Law Enforcement & Legal Authorities Disclosed only when strictly required by applicable law, court subpoena, or to protect the vital safety of users and venue staff.
🗄️

5. Data Retention

We retain your profile data for as long as you maintain an active Food Bazar account. Event invitations, past order histories, and table bookings are preserved to help you re-order favorites and manage booking histories. Financial transaction logs are archived in accordance with standard statutory, accounting, and tax compliance obligations before being securely deleted or anonymized.

🛡️

6. Your Rights and Permissions Control

  • Location Permission: You can enable or disable GPS location anytime through your device settings. If disabled, you can still search for venues manually by typing area names.
  • Camera Permission: Camera access is strictly requested for QR code scanning. You may revoke camera permission at any time in your phone's system settings.
  • Invitations & Event Visibility: Events you create can only be viewed by individuals with whom you explicitly share the invitation code/link.
  • Notification Preferences: Push notifications for live food tracking and marketing alerts can be toggled via app or system settings.
  • Access & Rectification: You may edit your profile information, saved addresses, and preferences directly in the app.
🗑️

7. Account and Data Deletion

You hold full ownership of your data. To permanently delete your Food Bazar account, send an email from your registered email address to:

apps@plexus.com.bd with the subject "Delete My Food Bazar Account".

All personal identifiers will be permanently purged from our active databases within 30 days, excluding mandatory tax or audit transaction records required by local law.
🔒

8. Data Security

We employ industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards. All network data transmissions between the App and our cloud servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Critical credentials and tokens are stored in protected, access-controlled environments.

👶

9. Children's Privacy

The Food Bazar platform is not intended for unsupervised children under 13 years of age. We do not intentionally collect personal data from minors. If a parent or guardian discovers that a minor has registered an account without consent, please contact us for immediate profile deletion.

🔗

10. Third-Party Links & Integrations

The App integrates third-party tools such as mapping solutions (e.g., Google Maps) and payment gateway providers. We advise you to read their independent privacy policies, as their processing protocols remain under their respective terms.

🔄
